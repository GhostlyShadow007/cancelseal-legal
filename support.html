Common questions

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for CancelSeal in your phone's settings. Some phones (Samsung, Xiaomi and others) stop apps in the background to save battery. Set CancelSeal's battery usage to "Unrestricted" so reminders arrive on time: Settings → Make sure reminders arrive shows the steps for your phone and opens the right page. Reminders go off 7, 3 and 1 days before the check date and on the day itself, at 09:00 unless you choose another time (in Settings, or per proof).

Where is my data? Can you restore it?

Everything is stored only on your phone. We have no copy and no account, so we can't restore lost data for you. Use Settings → Export backup and keep the file somewhere safe. To bring it back, use Settings → Restore from backup; the app saves a safety backup of your current vault first. Photos aren't stored in backups.

I turned on app lock. What if I can't unlock?

App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen PIN, pattern or password, so unlocking works the same way as unlocking your phone. CancelSeal has no separate password and we can't unlock it for you.

Does CancelSeal cancel subscriptions for me?

No. CancelSeal helps you keep evidence that you cancelled and reminds you to check for a re-charge. CancelSeal itself doesn't connect to your bank or to any service. Cancel now walks you through cancelling yourself and logs each step, and the built-in "How to cancel" guides show general steps for some popular services.

A cancellation guide looks out of date

Companies change their cancel pages often. Each guide shows when it was last checked; if the steps don't match what you see, follow the provider's own help page. You can tell us which guide is wrong at the address above.

How is the dispute deadline worked out?

When you answer "Got charged", CancelSeal counts from that day. It uses a 120-day window unless you change it for that proof (tap the pencil on the deadline panel). Banks and card schemes set their own time limits, so check yours with your bank. You get reminders 30 and 7 days before the last day.

How do the Dispute Pack and dispute email work?

Open a proof and tap Dispute Pack. CancelSeal builds an A4 PDF on your phone with the facts, timeline and your attached files, and you can share, save or print it. Dispute email opens a draft (Firm or Polite wording, fully editable) in your own email app with the pack attached. CancelSeal doesn't send the email: you review and send it yourself, and we never see it. Add the company's support address in the "To" field. If nothing opens, you may have no email app set up; use Copy and paste the text into one.

How do I add something from another app?

In the other app, tap Share and choose CancelSeal. It works for screenshots, PDFs, text and emails. You then choose whether it goes into a new proof or an existing one.

My widget says "Open CancelSeal" or "CancelSeal is locked"

Open CancelSeal once so the widget can fill in. With app lock turned on, widgets show only "CancelSeal is locked" so your amounts and service names don't appear on your home screen.
