Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · Updated for CancelSeal 1.3 · App: CancelSeal (Android) · Developer: The App Nest

Summary

CancelSeal is a local-only app. It helps you keep proof that you cancelled a subscription, free trial, refund, return or warranty, stored only on your own device. The app has no internet access, requires no account, and sends none of your data anywhere by itself. If you choose to share, save, print, export or email something, that content goes where you send it, through Android or the app you pick.

What we collect

Nothing. The App Nest does not collect, receive, transmit or share any personal data through CancelSeal. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no cloud service. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device. When you share, email or export something, you are the one sending it, and The App Nest does not receive a copy.

What the app stores on your device

  • Proof records: the service name, type, status, and the optional details you enter: cancellation date, re-charge check date and reminder times, amount and its currency, who bills you (for example Google Play or your bank), confirmation number, the name of the person you spoke to, how you cancelled, and notes. For a free trial: when it started, the date of the first charge, and whether you want a warning before it. If you change a proof's dispute window, the number of days. The evidence items you tick yourself on the evidence meter. Stored in a private on-device database.
  • Re-charge check answers: when you answer "Not charged" or "Got charged", the answer and the time you gave it are saved with the proof, to build its evidence trail.
  • Cancel now log: if you use "Cancel now", the steps you took, the problems you logged (for example "stuck in a bot or chat loop") and when, saved with the proof. Anything you capture during it is stored like any other attachment.
  • Attached images: if you attach screenshots or photos, a copy of each is saved in the app's private storage, made smaller (at most 2048 pixels on the long side) to save space. Your original images are not changed.
  • Attached files: if you attach a PDF, a saved email or a text file (up to 30 MB each), a copy is saved in the app's private storage. PDFs are shown by Android's own PDF renderer inside the app. Your original files are not changed.
  • Dispute Packs: when you create one, the app builds an A4 PDF on your phone from a proof (its facts, timeline, notes and attached files) and keeps it as a temporary file in its private cache folder. It stays there until Android clears the cache, you create a pack for the same proof again, or you clear the app's storage or uninstall. It is not put in your gallery or Downloads unless you choose Save.
  • Home-screen widgets: so Android can draw them, the app stores a small summary in its private storage: your protected total, a status line, and the names and dates of your next few re-charge checks. A widget you place on your home screen can be seen by anyone who sees that screen. With app lock on, the widgets show only "CancelSeal is locked".
  • Settings: your reminder time, your app lock and app switcher choices, and whether you have seen the welcome screens.
  • Backups: when you tap "Export backup" in Settings, the app writes a readable backup file in its own folder on your device. Before restoring a backup, the app first saves a safety backup of your current vault in the same folder. The app does not upload backups; you decide if and where to copy them. Backups hold your proof details, not the images.

None of this leaves your device unless you export, share, save, print or email it yourself.

Built into the app

  • Cancellation guides: the "How to cancel" guides are part of the app itself. Opening one loads nothing from the internet and tells no one which guide you read. A guide shows the provider's website as plain text; the app doesn't open it.
  • Calendar, evidence meter and dispute deadline: worked out on your phone from your own proofs.
  • Default currency: the app reads your phone's region setting to pick the currency it suggests for a new proof. That setting stays on your device.

Sharing into CancelSeal

CancelSeal appears in Android's share sheet for images, PDFs, text and emails. When you share something to it from another app, CancelSeal receives only what you chose to share. It keeps a temporary copy in its private cache (only the latest share is kept) and, if you save it, adds it to a new or existing proof. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Sharing out: Dispute Pack and email

Share, Save and Print on a Dispute Pack, and "Send via email app" on a dispute email, hand a file or text to Android or to an app you pick: the share sheet, Android's "Save to…" picker, Android's print service, or your own email app. CancelSeal prepares an email draft (To, Subject, text and the PDF) but does not send it. You review and send it from your email app, and it travels through your email provider under that provider's terms. The address and text you type for a draft are not saved by CancelSeal. The App Nest receives nothing.

To open your draft, CancelSeal asks Android which apps on your phone can handle email links. The answer stays on your device. This is not a permission.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, CancelSeal asks Android to confirm it's you with your fingerprint, face or device PIN, pattern or password. Android checks this itself: CancelSeal does not see, store or send your fingerprint, face data or PIN; it only learns whether the check succeeded. "Hide in app switcher" stops the app's screen from showing in recent apps and in screenshots.

Permissions and why they're used

  • Notifications: only to show reminders, trial warnings and dispute-deadline reminders. Asked for only when you save a reminder or answer "Got charged".
  • Run at startup: to re-arm reminders you already set after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: so a reminder notification can vibrate.
  • Photo picker / camera: only when you choose to attach an image. The system photo picker gives the app access only to the images you pick.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on app lock, to show Android's own unlock prompt (see App lock above).
  • Files you choose: when you attach a PDF or file, or restore a backup with "Choose a file", Android's file picker gives the app only the one file you pick. No storage permission is used.
  • No internet permission: the app has no network capability.

Sharing into the app, Dispute Packs, email drafts, cancellation guides, the calendar and home-screen widgets need no additional permission.

Links in the app (this policy, support, data deletion, and Google Play's subscriptions page) open in your web browser or the Play Store app. That app, not CancelSeal, loads those pages. The battery setting shortcut opens Android's own settings for CancelSeal.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting a proof removes it and its attached images and files. Temporary copies (shared-in files and Dispute Packs) stay in the app's cache until Android clears it; you can clear it yourself in Android's settings without losing your proofs. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data, including those copies and the widget summary. Files you saved, shared or emailed elsewhere are outside the app and yours to delete. There is no account and no copy on any server. See Data deletion.

Children

CancelSeal collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current version of CancelSeal. If a future version changes any of it, for example by adding advertising or an internet connection, we will update this page and its effective date, and update the Data safety information on Google Play, before that version is released.
