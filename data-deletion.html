Data deletion

CancelSeal (Android) · Developer: The App Nest

All CancelSeal data is stored only on your device. There is no account, and The App Nest has no copy of your data on any server, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Delete one proof

  1. Open CancelSeal and tap the proof.
  2. Tap the delete (bin) button and confirm.

The proof, its attached images and its reminder are removed from your device.

Delete everything

Uninstall CancelSeal. Android removes all of the app's data, including proofs, images, reminders and backups saved in the app's own folder.

Backup files you copied somewhere else (another folder, a computer or a cloud drive) are yours. Delete those yourself if you no longer want them.
