Data deletion

CancelSeal (Android) · Developer: The App Nest

CancelSeal keeps your data only on your device. There is no account, and The App Nest has no copy of your data on any server, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Delete one proof

  1. Open CancelSeal and tap the proof.
  2. Tap the delete (bin) button and confirm.

The proof, its attached images and files, and its reminders are removed from your device. A Dispute Pack you created from it is a separate temporary file (see below).

Delete temporary files

Files you shared into CancelSeal and Dispute Packs you created are kept as temporary copies in the app's private cache, not in your gallery or Downloads. To remove them without deleting your proofs, open Android Settings → Apps → CancelSeal → Storage and tap Clear cache. Do not tap "Clear data" unless you want to delete everything.

Delete everything

Uninstall CancelSeal. Android removes all of the app's data, including proofs, images, attached files, reminders, temporary copies, the home-screen widget summary and backups saved in the app's own folder (including the safety backups made before a restore). Remove any CancelSeal widgets from your home screen too.

Copies you saved or sent elsewhere are outside the app and yours to delete: a backup file you copied to another folder, a computer or a cloud drive, a Dispute Pack you saved, printed to a file, shared or attached to an email, and the email itself in your email app.
